How to use this guide

Follow the numbered steps. Gold boxes are tips. Brown boxes are important rules. Menu paths look like Packages or Invoices → New invoice.

Purely Eve Partner PortalSection 01

1. Sign in & menu

Open the Partner Portal link from Purely Eve and sign in with the email and password you registered.

Dashboard Inventory Customers Invoices Fulfillments Packages Payments Reports Profile

Tip

Use Sign out when finished, especially on a shared computer.

2. First-time setup (profile + Stripe)

Confirm your ship-from address

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Check your mailing and fulfillment (ship-from) address. Tax and shipping rates use this.
  3. Save any updates.

Connect Stripe (required to get paid)

  1. Open Payments.
  2. Start Stripe Connect onboarding and complete the Stripe-hosted steps (bank details, identity).
  3. When charges and payouts are enabled, customer payments can land in your account.

Fees

Customers pay you. Stripe’s card fee (typically 2.9% + $0.30) comes out of your proceeds. Shipping the customer pays on the invoice goes to Purely Eve to fund the postage label.

Before you can buy packages

Purely Eve must approve your registration and accept your resale certificate. You will get email when package purchasing is unlocked.

Purely Eve Partner PortalSection 03

3. Buy inventory packages

  1. Go to Packages.
  2. Choose a package (for example Starter or Growth) and continue to checkout.
  3. Confirm ship-to details and choose a shipping rate.
  4. Pay through Stripe. Inventory is credited when payment completes.
  5. Purely Eve ships the package to you and may email tracking when it goes out.

Tip

Package purchases are prepaid and not taxed (resale). You charge tax on retail customer sales.

4. Inventory on hand

Open Inventory to see units on hand. Stock increases when a package order is paid and decreases when a customer invoice is paid / fulfilled per portal rules. Low stock is highlighted so you know when to reorder packages.

5. Customers

  1. Go to Customers → add a customer.
  2. Enter name, email, phone, and billing / shipping addresses.
  3. For wholesale (business) customers, capture resale certificate number and upload when required.
Purely Eve Partner PortalSection 06

6. Create & send invoices

  1. Go to Invoices → New invoice.
  2. Select the customer and customer type: Direct to Customer (retail / intro pricing with tax) or Retail Wholesale (business pricing, typically untaxed with resale docs).
  3. Add line items from your assigned products. Prices fill in automatically — you cannot edit price.
  4. Choose shipping (live rates) or free shipping if offered.
  5. Review totals (product, shipping, tax) and send. The customer gets a branded payment link email.

Unpaid invoice

You can resend the payment link or cancel before they pay.

After they pay

Status becomes paid. Open Fulfillments to buy a label and ship.

Purely Eve Partner PortalSection 07

7. Fulfillment, labels & refunds

  1. Open Fulfillments (or the link from a paid invoice).
  2. Buy the shipping label. Postage is covered by shipping already collected on the invoice.
  3. Print the label, ship the order, and the customer receives tracking by email.
  4. You can reprint the label later from the same order page.

Refund a paid customer order

  1. Open the paid invoice or its fulfillment page.
  2. Use Refund & restore stock.
  3. The customer is refunded through Stripe and inventory is put back on hand.

Note

If a label was already purchased, shipping that went to Purely Eve for postage may not be returned (postage was spent). Refund the customer product/tax share as shown in the portal.

Purely Eve Partner PortalSection 08

8. Payouts & reports

Payments

Under Payments you can see Stripe Connect status, available / pending balance, and recent payouts. Open the payout report for fee detail on paid invoices.

Reports

  1. Open Reports for month, year-to-date, and lifetime sales.
  2. Filter by date range and use Export report for a CSV download.
  3. Click Tax collected to open tax by ship-to state (also exportable).
