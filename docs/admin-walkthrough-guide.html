Latest submissions — click a business name to open the Partner file.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 03

3. Approving Partners & resale documents

Partners register in the portal (after coming through your Shopify application flow). They accept the Partner Agreement and upload a resale certificate during signup. You still review and activate them here.

Approve a registration

  1. Go to Distributors.
  2. Open the Partner with status Pending.
  3. Review contact details, business info, and agreement acceptance.
  4. Choose Approve (or Decline with a note if needed).

Accept the resale certificate (required before packages)

  1. On the same Partner page, open the uploaded resale document (view / download).
  2. If it looks correct, mark it Accepted.
  3. If it is wrong or incomplete, reject it so the Partner can re-upload.

Business rule

A Partner cannot purchase inventory packages until (1) their registration is approved and (2) their resale certificate is accepted. Inventory package orders are prepaid before you ship.

Suspend or remove

On an approved Partner you can Suspend or Remove access. Deleting a Partner is permanent — only use when you are sure.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 04

4. Products & inventory packages

Under Products you manage two catalogs: consumer products Partners sell to customers, and inventory packages Partners buy from the company.

Consumer products

Name, description, image, weight, and three prices you set: retail, wholesale, and introductory. Partners cannot edit price.

Inventory packages

Starter (20 units), Growth (40 units), plus any future packages you add. Price, unit count, weight/dims, image, and active flag.

Add or edit a consumer product

  1. Go to Products → Add product (or open an existing one).
  2. Enter SKU, name, description, image, weight, and the three prices.
  3. Save. The product is available for Partner invoices according to visibility rules below.

Add or edit an inventory package

  1. On Products, use the inventory packages section → add or edit.
  2. Set name, SKU, unit count (how many serums the package credits), price, shipping weight/dims, image.
  3. Leave Active on when Partners should be able to buy it.

Tip — $1 test package

For Stripe practice, use a low-price test package. Deactivate or hide it before real Partner purchases so nobody buys the test SKU by mistake.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 05

5. Who can see which products (assignments)

Each product and package can be visible to all Partners, or limited to selected Partners.

  1. Open the product or package detail page.
  2. If “visible to all” is checked, every approved Partner can see it.
  3. If you uncheck it, the list starts empty — nobody is assigned yet.
  4. Search Partners (alphabetical list) and assign only who should see that SKU or package.

When to use limited visibility

Use this for intro-only offers, regional pilots, or special packages that should not appear in every Partner’s catalog.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 06

6. Inventory package orders (fulfillment)

When a Partner pays for a Starter / Growth (or other) package, the order appears under Inventory orders. You ship from the company side; labels are purchased on the company EasyPost account.

  1. Go to Inventory orders.
  2. Filter by status (e.g. Paid), date, or Partner name / order number.
  3. Open a paid order and buy / print the shipping label.
  4. Mark fulfilled when shipped. Stock is credited to the Partner when payment completes.

Shipping model (locked)

Company pays EasyPost for inventory-package labels to Partners. For Partner → customer orders, Partners collect shipping on the invoice; that shipping amount comes to the company (application fee) and the company buys the customer label.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 07

7. Reports (the seven admin reports)

Open Reports from the top menu. Each report supports filters; new packages appear automatically.

# Report What you get
1 Partner inventory package sales Per Partner: date ordered, amount, volume. Flags Partners with no package order in 90+ days. Filter by name and dates.
2 Company-wide package volume Month, YTD, lifetime units/volume. Filter by Partner, dates, and package (e.g. 20 vs 40 units).
3 Partner customer orders Retail vs wholesale per Partner; month / YTD / lifetime. Click an invoice for customer + line items.
4 Company-wide customer volume All Partner customer sales (excludes inventory packages). Filter retail / wholesale / dates.
5 Tax collected Tax by Partner and ship-to state for month, YTD, or custom range.
6 Shipping collected Customer shipping collected for the company, with fulfilled vs pending label status.
7 Company Stripe Platform balance, shipping fees, package sales on the company account, recent payouts.

Partner reports

Partners also have a Reports page for their own sales, tax, and CSV download. They never see network-wide admin totals.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 08

8. Company Stripe view

Path: Admin → Reports → Company Stripe.

  • Available / Pending — platform Stripe balance.
  • Shipping collected — amounts taken from Partner checkouts for postage.
  • Inventory packages — paid package volume on the company account for the selected period.
  • Balance transactions & payouts — recent activity (full ledger also lives in the Stripe Dashboard).

Reminder

Customer card payments land on each Partner’s connected Stripe account. The company receives shipping application fees and inventory-package payments. Card processing fees on customer sales are absorbed by the Partner.

Purely Eve Partner Portal Section 09

9. Go-live checklist (test → live)

  1. Complete a full dry run in Stripe test mode with the $1 test package (pay, label, stock credit).
  2. Confirm Stripe Tax registrations for states you sell into (Test first, then Live).
  3. Confirm EasyPost company key and label buying work on a paid package order.
  4. Switch portal env keys to Stripe live only when ready for real money.
  5. Deactivate the $1 test package before Partners place real Starter / Growth orders.
  6. Have one trusted Partner place a small live package order and verify payout + fulfillment.

Do not skip

Never mix test and live keys. After switching to live, run one controlled real purchase before inviting the full Partner group.
